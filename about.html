I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in Bayesian Statistics and High-Performance Computing at KAUST, working under the supervision of Professor David Keyes and Professor Håvard Rue.

I lead the development of sTiles, an accelerated HPC framework for sparse matrix computations powering R-INLA, and PyINLA, a Python interface for fast Bayesian inference using the INLA methodology.

My work is conducted in close collaboration with Dr. Hatem Ltaief, focusing on high-performance computing (HPC) techniques for advancing Bayesian inference methodologies.
